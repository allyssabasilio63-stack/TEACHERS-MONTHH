Hello Ma,am!

Happy Teacher's Month po!

First and foremost, thank you so much for being there for me and Ivan during our duty sa office. I know hindi long enough yung pagsasama natin doon, but that experience was a big change for me.

Thank you for listening to me whenever I have rants sa ibang subjects. Thanks for listening din whenever may chika ako na unrelated sa subject niyo. Thank you for helping us sa ibang sub. I really, really appreciate that po, sobra!

You made us feel na welcome kami sa office, and I’m so grateful for that. Palagi ko talagang tini-treasure and cherish yung situation na yun because, for us, it became more than just a simple duty sa office.

Thank you, Ma’am! Hindi lang sa pagiging teacher and student yung naging memories natin. Nagkaroon kami ni Ivan ng mapagsasabihan, someone who would listen to our rants and chikas, and most importantly, we also gained a true friend.

I’m really grateful that we got to experience that with you, Ma’am. Thank you for making us feel comfortable, welcome, and appreciated. I will always cherish those moments with you. 💜

With love and gratitude,
Ali 💜
